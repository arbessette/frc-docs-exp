CAN Wiring Basics
=================

Keep CAN cables supported near their connectors and protected from moving
parts. Record device IDs with the robot's wiring notes.

For Systemcore CAN connectors, bus layout, and termination, continue to
`Systemcore CAN Wiring in WPILib
<{{ wpilib_docs_url }}docs/software/systemcore-info/systemcore-can-wiring.html>`_.

For the drivetrain example, use the `Systemcore wiring guide
<{{ wpilib_docs_url }}docs/zero-to-robot/systemcore-wiring.html>`_.
